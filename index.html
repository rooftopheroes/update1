Questions, Answered

Roofing questions homeowners ask us.

Is the roof inspection really free?

Yes. We inspect your roof, document what we find with photos, and give you a written estimate with clear options. There is no charge, no obligation, and no pressure - if your roof has years left in it, we'll tell you.

How does the instant online quote work?

Enter your address in our instant quote tool and it measures your roof from aerial imagery to show real price ranges for top-rated roof options in seconds, with no phone call required. When you're ready, we confirm the details with a free on-site inspection.

What areas do you serve?

Our office is at 3027 Miller Rd in Ann Arbor. We serve Washtenaw County, western Wayne County, Livingston County, and select Oakland County communities, including Ypsilanti, Saline, Dexter, Chelsea, Plymouth, Canton, Northville, Brighton, Howell, Whitmore Lake, Pinckney, Manchester, Milan, Dundee, Novi, Farmington Hills, West Bloomfield, Bloomfield Hills and Birmingham.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Rooftop Heroes LLC is a licensed Michigan residential builder (License #262600515) and fully insured. We are an Owens Corning Preferred Contractor and certified with GAF and CertainTeed.

What roofing brands and materials do you install?

We install premium architectural asphalt shingle systems from Owens Corning, CertainTeed and GAF, built for Michigan winters, along with the underlayment, ice-and-water barrier, ventilation and flashing that make a roof last. Your estimate spells out exactly which products are included.

Do you help with storm damage and insurance claims?

Yes. If wind, hail or a fallen branch damaged your roof, we document the damage with photos, provide a detailed estimate, and help you understand the paperwork for your insurance claim. You can upload photos and insurance documents when you book your inspection online.

How long does a roof replacement take?

Most residential roof replacements are completed in one to two days, depending on the size and pitch of the roof and the weather. We protect your landscaping and finish every job with a full cleanup, including a magnetic sweep for nails.

Do you offer financing?

Yes. Monthly payment options are available through our financing partner, and you can check your options without a hard credit pull. Ask us during your inspection or use the Explore Financing Options button above.

What warranty comes with a new roof?

Qualifying roof systems are backed by the Owens Corning Preferred Protection warranty, and our work is covered by a workmanship warranty. We walk you through exactly what is covered, in writing, before you sign anything.

How do I get started?

Book a free inspection online in about 30 seconds, call or text (734) 201-1000, or get an instant quote. You can also stop by our office in Ann Arbor - we'd love to meet you.
